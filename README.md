# Travel and Hospitality Analytics — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive five-page Power BI report analyzing revenue, bookings, cancellations, guest demographics, and hotel operations across a **City Hotel** and a **Resort Hotel** (2015–2017).

![Dashboard Preview].(Snapshot%20of%20Dashboard.png).

## Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw hotel booking records into actionable insights for hotel management, covering financial performance, cancellation risk, customer loyalty, and operational demand.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Tracks revenue, Average Daily Rate (ADR), lead time, and bookings by market segment, month, and distribution channel.
* **Channel Concentration:** 56.65% of total revenue (€23.92M) comes from Online Travel Agents (Online TA).
* **Seasonality:** Revenue peaks in August (€7.9M) and drops to its lowest point in January (~€1.3M).
* **Distribution:** The TA/TO channel generates €36M compared to €5M from Direct bookings.

### 2. Booking Retention Analytics
Examines cancellations, deposit types, lead times, and special requests to identify cancellation risk.
* **Overall Cancellations:** 37.38% cancellation rate (44K cancelled out of 117K bookings).
* **Lead Time Impact:** Cancelled bookings average a 145-day lead time versus 80 days for kept bookings.
* **Special Requests:** Bookings with zero special requests have a 48.35% cancellation rate, dropping to 5% for bookings with five special requests.

### 3. Guest Demographics
Analyzes guest origins, customer types, and party composition (adults, children, and babies).
* **Guest Volume:** 232K guests across 117K bookings from dozens of countries, heavily concentrated in Europe.
* **Customer Type:** Transient customers generate 82.39% of total revenue (€33.94M).
* **Loyalty Gap:** Only 2.95% of guests are repeat visitors; adults make up the vast majority of guests.

### 4. Hotel Operations
Monitors room type demand, car parking requirements, and booking modifications.
* **Room Demand:** Room Type A accounts for 84K bookings, far ahead of Room Type D (19K).
* **Parking:** 93.74% of bookings require no parking space.
* **Booking Changes:** 25K total booking changes, predominantly occurring on Room Type A.

### 5. Booking Details (Drill-Through)
Provides an individual booking-level table accessible by right-clicking any chart in the report.
* Displays Booking ID, Hotel, Country, Market Segment, Reserved Room Type, Arrival Date, and Total Revenue.
* Reconciles directly to the €42.22M grand total shown on the Executive Overview.

## Key Findings & Recommendations

1. **Reduce Channel Dependence:** Online TA brings in 57% of revenue. Grow Direct and Corporate channels through targeted loyalty offers and partnerships.
2. **Flatten Seasonality:** Revenue peaks in August and slumps from November to January. Introduce off-season packages and group deals to stabilize demand.
3. **Cut Cancellations:** Flag long lead-time bookings, encourage deposits, and engage guests early to reduce the 37% cancellation rate.
4. **Build Repeat Guests:** With only a 2.95% repeat guest rate, launching a structured loyalty programme can drive more stable, recurring revenue.
